title: NPCs
description: Non-player characters that loop an idle clip, open a dialog, and can be recruitable
---

NPCs are world inhabitants that are not controlled by the player and pose no threat. They belong to the `neutral` team and are rendered via `Actor.vue`.

## Characteristics

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| `type` | `character` |
| `subtype` | `npc` |
| `team` | `neutral` |
| `controllable` | `false` |
| Component | `Actor.vue` |
| Behavior | `useActorBehavior` loops `ai.idleAnimation` (default `IDLE_A`) |
| Dialog | optional `dialogId` |
| Recruitment | optional `recruitable: true` |

## Visual Distinctions

:::div{class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-6 items-start"}

- **Nameplate on hover**: live portrait thumbnail, white name, `Lv. X · Race` when defined, HP bar, armor bars when the NPC has armor
- **Outline on hover**: the `neutral` outline preset (white)
- **No target indicator**: not selectable for movement
- **Right-click**: opens the entity context menu

:::

## Stats

NPCs carry full combat stats even if not engaged in combat, reflecting their role and rank in the world:

```yaml
stats:
  hp: 45
  maxHp: 45
  ac: 18
  strength: 16
  dexterity: 12
  constitution: 14
  intelligence: 10
  wisdom: 12
  charisma: 14
```

## Idle animation

The only `ai` field the engine reads is `idleAnimation`. `useActorBehavior` validates it against `AnimationName` and falls back to `IDLE_A`:

```yaml
ai:
  behavior: idle
  idleAnimation: Idle_B
```

::note
`behavior: idle` is stored on the entity but nothing branches on it. `recruitEntity` and `dismissEntity` rewrite it (`companion` / `idle`) as bookkeeping. There is no wander, patrol or dialogue AI.
::

## Dialog

`dialogId` names the dialog graph the [dialog engine](/dialog/overview) opens for this NPC. Zynrae uses it for her recruitment conversation (`zynrae_alley`), Captain Aldric for `guard_captain_gate`.

## Recruitable NPCs

An NPC with `recruitable: true` appears in `gameStore.recruitableEntities` and can join the party through `gameStore.recruitEntity(id)`. Zynrae starts life as one:

```yaml
# companions/zynrae.yaml (trimmed)
templateId: zynrae
team: neutral
controllable: false
recruitable: true
subtype: npc
dialogId: zynrae_alley
ai:
  behavior: idle
```

Once recruited she is a companion; `dismissEntity` turns her back into this NPC. See [Companions](/actors/companions).

## Captain Aldric

Guard captain of the knight faction. Heavy armor, high AC, has a dialog.

```yaml
templateId: guard_captain
type: character
subtype: npc
name: Captain Aldric
level: 5
race: Human
faction: knight
team: neutral
controllable: false
ai:
  behavior: idle
  idleAnimation: Idle_B
dialogId: guard_captain_gate
portrait: /img/portraits/knight_portrait.png
model: /models/characters/Knight.glb
rig: Rig_Medium
```
