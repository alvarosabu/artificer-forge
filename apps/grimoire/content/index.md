#title
Engine Features

#features
  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-heroicons-cube
  ---
  #title
  Declarative 3D

  #description
  Build 3D scenes with Vue components using TresJS. No imperative Three.js boilerplate.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-heroicons-user
  ---
  #title
  Modular Characters

  #description
  Swap heads, hair and armor on a shared skeleton. Animation packs with crossfade and type-safe names.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-heroicons-puzzle-piece
  ---
  #title
  Engine Package

  #description
  One engine package with core rules, runtime scene components and HUD widgets. VFX and post-processing ship as siblings.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-heroicons-sun
  ---
  #title
  Stylized Environment

  #description
  TSL grading, day cycle, instanced grass and flowers, quadtree terrain and depth-buffer water on WebGPU.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-heroicons-beaker
  ---
  #title
  Playground First

  #description
  Prototype in playground, extract to packages when stable. Iterate fast.
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-simple-icons-nuxt
  ---
  #title
  Nuxt 4 Powered

  #description
  Full-stack Vue framework with SSR, auto-imports, and hybrid rendering.
  :::
